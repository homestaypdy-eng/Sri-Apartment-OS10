# Sri Apartment OS — Staff Manual

## Logging In

Use the email and password your admin created for you. If you forget your password, ask an admin to reset it — there's no self-service password reset yet.

## Daily Workflow

1. Dashboard — see today's arrivals/departures, dirty units, pending housekeeping tasks, and open maintenance tickets at a glance.
2. Bookings — create bookings, check availability, and move a booking through Check In then Check Out. Checking out automatically creates a cleaning task for that unit.
3. Housekeeping — see the room-status board (clean/dirty/inspected) and your task list. Mark a task in-progress, then complete — completing a task is what marks the room clean again.
4. Maintenance — raise a ticket for anything broken, assign priority, and update status as you work on it.
5. Inquiries — when someone asks about a stay, create an inquiry (quotation). Once they pay an advance, Convert to Booking turns it into a real, confirmed booking automatically — no need to re-enter their details.
6. Billing — record payments and generate GST invoices for confirmed bookings.
7. Inventory — record Stock In when supplies arrive, Stock Out when used. Low-stock items are flagged automatically.

## Sending a Quotation

From Inquiries, open a quotation and use Send WhatsApp or Send Email:
- WhatsApp opens a pre-filled chat with the quotation summary. You still need to press send, and since WhatsApp links can't auto-attach files, download the PDF first and attach it manually in the chat.
- Email attaches the PDF automatically, if your admin has configured email sending. If not, you'll see a clear not-configured message rather than a silent failure.

## Guest Requests & Complaints

Guests can log into their own portal (a link you or an admin sends them) and raise service requests, complaints, or stay-extension requests. Check Guest Requests regularly and update status as you address them (open, then in_progress, then resolved).

## Follow-ups

The Inquiries page shows a Follow-ups Due widget for leads you've marked for a callback. This only shows what's due when you open the page — it doesn't push a notification to you, so make checking it part of your routine.

## What You Can't Do (Ask an Admin)

- Create or deactivate user accounts
- Change business Settings (bank details, company info)
- Approve your own leave requests
- Generate or mark payroll as paid
- Issue refunds
- View audit logs
